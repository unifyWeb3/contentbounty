ContentBounty v2.3 milestone proof — challenge evidence

This document is the published evidence source for the pre-payout challenge
filed against the milestone submission on GenLayer Studionet. It is served
over canonical HTTPS from raw.githubusercontent.com and is rendered by the
GenVM web renderer during the challenge review consensus round.

Subject: a rights and plagiarism dispute about the milestone submission.

The challenger states that the submitted document was published by the
challenger before the submitting wallet claimed it, and that the submitting
wallet therefore has no prior authorship of the material. The challenger
records the publication date of the earlier copy and asks the review round to
compare the two texts.

The challenger also states that the earlier copy and the submitted document
differ only in the appended wallet claim tag, which is the same observation a
reviewer would make when comparing a claimed copy with an earlier public
copy of the same text.

The GenLayer evidence commitment for this document is the SHA-256 of its
normalized text, recomputed independently by every GenLayer validator that
participates in the review round.
